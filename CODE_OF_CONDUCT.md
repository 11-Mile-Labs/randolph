# Code of conduct

Randolph welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Help make the project a place where people can ask questions, disagree, and learn.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and assume good intent while responding to the actual impact of your words.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Welcome questions, explain feedback, and respect others' time.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information, private conversations, or credentials without permission.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces maintained by the project, including when someone represents Randolph publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact a project maintainer privately using a contact method listed on their GitHub profile. If no private channel is listed, ask for one in an issue without posting personal information or incident details. If the report concerns a maintainer, contact another maintainer when available. Never post sensitive evidence publicly to obtain attention.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban accounts depending on the behavior and its impact. They should explain moderation decisions to the affected person when safe, handle reports discreetly, and avoid participating in decisions about their own conduct. A small volunteer project cannot guarantee immediate responses.
